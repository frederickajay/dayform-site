Frequently asked questions

Is my data backed up?

Your journal lives on your iPhone and is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups. You can also create a full backup in Settings → Your library, and export PDF or Excel files from the Trends tab.

How does the free trial work?

Every feature is free for 10 days. After that, logging new entries needs the monthly membership. You can always view and export what you've logged. Manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings → Membership → Restore purchases in Dayform.

The Apple Watch app shows no exercises.

Open Dayform on your iPhone once, with your Watch nearby, so your recent exercises sync to it.

How do I turn features off?

Settings → Features lets you switch off anything you don't use.
