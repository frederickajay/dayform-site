Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours

Effective 14 November 2026

Dayform has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you log is stored on your device. We don't collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Meals, nutrition, workouts, weight, water, steps, journal text, voice notes, photos, report cards and settings are saved only on your iPhone (and on your Apple Watch, which syncs with your iPhone directly).
  • Voice notes and the evening diary are turned into text on your device where your iPhone supports it. Apple may process speech if on-device recognition isn't available for your language.
  • Food photos, menus and progress photos are analysed on your device.
  • Reminders and the 7 PM nudge are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

Optional features that connect, only when you use them

  • Apple Health: if you allow it, Dayform reads steps, active energy and weight, and can write nutrition and weight. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your device through Dayform.
  • Online food search and barcodes: when a food isn't in the built-in database, the search text or barcode is sent to the Open Food Facts database to look it up. No personal information is included.
  • iCloud sync (if enabled in a future version): your journal would be stored in your private iCloud database, which only you can access.
  • Premium photo analysis (if available to members): the photo you choose is sent securely to Dayform's analysis service and an AI model to identify foods, and is not stored.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled by Apple. Dayform only receives confirmation that your membership is active.

Sharing and exports

Report cards, backups, PDF and Excel exports are created on your device and only leave it when you choose to share or save them.

Children

Dayform isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored by it. Data you shared with Apple Health stays in Health until you remove it there.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if anything changes. Questions? See Support.
