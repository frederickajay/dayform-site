Questions

Is Dayform free?

Dayform is free to download and every feature is free for 10 days. After that, Dayform Membership ($4.99 per month) keeps logging open. Your entries always stay viewable and exportable.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. Dayform has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Does Dayform work with Apple Health and Apple Watch?

Yes. Dayform reads steps, active energy and weight from Apple Health, and the Apple Watch app logs sets, rest and water.

Can I count calories and macros?

Yes. Search over 8,000 foods, scan a barcode, snap a photo or just say what you ate, and Dayform totals calories, protein, carbs, fat and water.
