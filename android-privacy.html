Privacy Policy · Android

Your data stays yours

Dayform for Android. Effective 22 November 2026

Dayform for Android is provided by Sanny Samuel. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you log is stored on your phone. We don't sell or share your journal or health records.

What stays on your phone

  • Meals, nutrition, workouts, weight, water, steps, journal text, voice notes, photos, videos, report cards, vows, alarms and settings are saved only in Dayform's private storage on your phone (and on your Wear OS watch, which talks to your phone directly).
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Dayform's data.
  • Wake-up alarms, reminders and the evening nudge are scheduled on your phone. Songs and pictures you choose for an alarm are copied into Dayform's private storage and never leave the phone.
  • Food photos, menus and progress photos are analysed on your phone where it supports this.

Optional features that connect, only when you use them

  • Health Connect: if you allow it, Dayform reads steps, active calories and weight, and can write nutrition and weight. This data is never used for advertising and never leaves your phone through Dayform.
  • Online food search and barcodes: when a food isn't in the built-in database, the search text or barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts database to look it up. No personal information is included.
  • Voice: speech is turned into text on your phone where it can be. If you turn on online speech recognition, Google's speech service may process it.
  • Private sync: if you turn it on, your journal is stored in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which only you can access. Dayform has no server for your journal.
  • Online photo analysis: only if you agree each time, the photo you choose is sent securely to Dayform's analysis service and an AI model to identify foods, and is not stored.

Purchases and membership

Google Play handles subscription payments; Dayform never sees your card details. To confirm a membership, the app sends the Google Play purchase token to Dayform's membership service, hosted on Google Cloud Run, which checks it with Google Play and acknowledges new purchases. The service receives no journal or health records and does not keep a database of purchase tokens. Google Cloud keeps standard request logs (time, IP address and technical details) for up to 30 days to keep the service running and secure.

Sharing and exports

Report cards, backups, PDF and spreadsheet exports are created on your phone and only leave it when you choose to share or save them. Backup files are not encrypted, so choose a private place for them.

Children

Dayform isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall Dayform to remove everything it stores on your phone. Copies you exported, synced to your Google Drive or wrote to Health Connect stay there until you remove them. There's no Dayform account to delete. Uninstalling doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play.
